# REG4v2
A 4-bit register with a {write-enable} (`LOAD`) input.It stores 4 bits and 
either:
- **Loads** the value on `D` when `LOAD = 1` 
- **Holds** its current value when `LOAD = 0`

Built from:
- 4× D flip-flops (storage)
- 4× 2:1 multiplexers (load/hold selection)
- 2× splitters (bus fan-out and merge)
